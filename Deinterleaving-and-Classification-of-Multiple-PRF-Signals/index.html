Deinterleaving and Classification of Multiple PRF Signals

Overview

Analyzing and identifying radar signals is a crucial objective in electronic support measures. Received signals often originate from multiple radars simultaneously. In modern electronic warfare, radars possess their own frequency parameters and switch between multiple sets of frequency parameters. The received signal may be a mixture of signals transmitted by high, medium, and low PRF radars. Separating these radar signals when their pulses overlap is a highly challenging task.

Key Contributions

  • QPSE Encoder: Solves the issue of varying input dimensions for neural networks by encoding radar pulse features.
  • Pulse Mixture Simulation: Simulates pulse missing, overlapping, and interleaving scenarios.
  • Multiscale Neural Network: A model capable of deinterleaving up to 15 mixed radar signals and classifying their modes.

Problem Description

Pulse Descriptor Words (PDWs)

PDWs describe the parameters of a radar Pulse Stream. The key parameters studied here are:

  • TOA: Time of arrival
  • PW: Pulse width
  • PRI: Pulse repetition interval
  • PRF: Pulse repetition frequency

A Pulse Stream can be expressed as TOA and PW sequences:

$$ TOA\ Sequence=[t_1,t_2,\ldots,t_i,\ldots,t_N] $$

$$ PW\ Sequence=[t_{t_1},t_{t_2},\ldots,t_{t_i},\ldots,t_{t_N}] $$

Pulse Missing & Jitter

Radars may employ PRI/PW Jitter or switch parameters to avoid detection. When TOAs from different radars arrive simultaneously, pulses can overlap, leading to missing data or corrupted features.

Methodology: QPSE & Multiscale Network

We propose the Quantized Pulse Stream Expression (QPSE) to handle the varying length of TOA sequences. This encodes frequency-domain TOA and PW sequences into a fixed-length quantized time-domain representation.
